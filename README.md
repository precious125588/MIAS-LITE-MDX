## ⚡ How to Setup in 60 Seconds

1. **Pair Your Phone**: Click the [Pairing Dashboard](https://web-production-0a742.up.railway.app/), enter your WhatsApp phone number with country code, and tap **Pair with Phone**.
2. **Copy Your Session ID**: Copy the generated universal session string (starts with `JUST_X~`).
3. **Deploy on Railway**:
   - In your Railway service settings, open the **Variables** tab.
   - Add environment variable:
     - `SESSION_ID` = `<your copied session string>`
     - *(Optional)* `OWNER_NUMBER` = `<your WhatsApp number>`
     - *(Optional)* `OWNER_NAME` = `𝑷𝑹𝑬𝑪𝑰𝑶𝑼𝑺 x`
4. **Deploy**: Hit **Redeploy**. Your logs will immediately display:
   ```
   🟢 [BOT ALIVE] WHATSAPP BOT IS ONLINE & CONNECTED!
   ```
